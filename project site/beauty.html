The beauty edit

Beauty favorites

I like makeup that feels natural and glowy, plus hair products that make styling a little easier. Here are four brands I love including in my routine.

Charlotte Tilbury
product photo
Makeup / Glow

Charlotte Tilbury

Hollywood Flawless Filter

I love the way this adds a pretty glow without making my makeup look too heavy. It works on its own or underneath other makeup when I want a more natural finish.

My pick for a glowy makeup look.
Rhode
product photo
Makeup / Cheeks & Lips

Rhode

Toasted Teddy blush + PBJ lip tint

Toasted Teddy gives a warm blush look, while PBJ adds a glossy pop of color to my lips. Both are easy to work into an everyday routine.

Easy color without a full makeup look.
L'Oréal Paris
product photo
Makeup / Eyes

L'Oréal Paris

Telescopic Original Mascara — Blackest Black

This is one of my favorite mascaras because I like how it makes my lashes look longer and more defined.

My go-to for defined lashes.
Not Your Mother's
product photo
Hair / Texture

Not Your Mother's

Beach Babe Texturizing Sea Salt Spray

I like using this when I want a more casual, textured beach-wave look instead of perfectly styled hair.

For effortless-looking texture.
Beauty note

Keep what works.

I don't think you need a huge collection of products to have a routine you enjoy. I would rather keep a few favorites that fit my style.
